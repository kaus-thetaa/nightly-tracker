## Rules
- Missing a day never restarts the plan: the next day picks up where it stopped.
- A problem counts only after you can re-solve it from scratch a day later; keep a short list of the ones that failed.
- One project gets finished before any new one starts. AeroMind and the F1 predictor wait until RaceLine AI is live.
- Sunday is a review and rest day: redo the week's failed problems, nothing new.
- Do not apply to any company until the resume has been updated (end of the Ship block).

## Notes
- The sheet has 108 listings across 91 companies and does not say which year or cohort they came from; this year's list can differ.
- Stipend currency and period are not stated, and durations are treated as months by assumption.
- Cutoffs are as listed; the sheet does not say whether they are checked against the CGPA after Sem 3 or after Sem 4. Confirm with the placement cell.
- A CGPA cutoff is a minimum to apply. It does not mean you will be shortlisted; most of these companies test coding first.
- The online coding test is the first filter at every company. The 400-problem target (about 2 a day from January) is aimed at clearing it.
